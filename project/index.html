This website covers the baseball career of Samuel (Sam) Leight.

The Leight Family

The Leight family consists of Samuel Leight and his parents James and Margaret.

Samuel was born in the Bay Area in San Ramon, California. The Leight's have lived in Torrance, CA for 23 years. Torrance is a Los Angeles suburb.

It is close to the Los Angeles Dodgers and the Los Angeles Angels.

Love of Baseball

The Leight family are True Blue Los Angeles Dodger fans. James and Margaret attended games when they were dating and first married. They started to take Samuel to the games when he was 3 years old.

They have attended many LA Dodger games and have watched MLB games at many ball parks throughout the United States.

Los Angeles Dodgers

The Los Angeles Dodgers are a National League West Division Team.
